ChopCal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

ChopCal respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your goal, height, weight, and target weight.
  • Meal records you log (dish name, calories, macros).
  • Steps and water intake you enter manually.
  • An anonymous device identifier.

How we use it

We use this data to estimate calories, build your progress trends, and personalize your daily plan. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Storage & analytics

Your data is stored locally on your device. We use Firebase only for anonymous analytics and crash reports to improve the app.

Permissions

Camera — used only to photograph and recognize your food. You can deny it anytime.
Notifications — optional reminders for logging meals and weekly weigh-ins.
Network — used for AI recognition, analytics, and crash reporting.
Health data (later) — steps sync via HealthKit / Google Fit, only with your permission.

Your control

You can review or update your information anytime in the app. Contact us at aykon1229@gmail.com for privacy questions.
